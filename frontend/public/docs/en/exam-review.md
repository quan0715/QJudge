# Reviewing Exam Content

Once an exam is published, modifications are restricted by student submission state and exam integrity policies. Instructors and TAs should always walk through the complete test from the student perspective before releasing it, ensuring layout, content, and policies align with course expectations.

## Opening the Answer Preview

On the **Exam Management** page for your exam, click the eye icon in the top right to **Preview Answer Screen**. This launches Demo Mode in a separate view, which will not generate student records or expose the exam to enrolled students prematurely.

![Teacher previewing the exam in Demo Mode](/docs/images/admin-getting-started/exam-preview.png)

*Figure: The left panel lists both questions while the center displays the student answering canvas. Demo Mode lets you check question types, order, options, and rendering before release.*

Switch between individual questions, and toggle between "Question-by-Question Mode" and "All Questions Mode". If questions include code snippets, tables, math equations, or images, verify their rendering in the preview rather than relying solely on the editor.

## Questions and Scoring Checklist

Check each question against the following:

- Question order follows pedagogical intent and question numbers are sequential.
- Question types, options, and selected correct answers are consistent.
- Individual question points and total score are accurate (5 + 15 = 20 points in this example).
- Essay questions have explicit rubrics and point distributions.
- Student instructions are complete, and instructor-only answers and explanations do not leak into the student interface.

If edits are needed, close the Demo tab, make adjustments in **Exam Management**, wait for "All changes saved", and re-open the preview to confirm.

## Roster and Eligibility

Return to the contest overview and verify that registered participants match your intended student group. In this example, only our three sample student accounts appear.

Classroom rosters exist at the course level, whereas exam participants define the scope of a single event. Add/drop changes, make-up tests, or joint sections may cause differences; verify these differences are intentional before publishing.

## Schedule and Exam Policies

Under **Settings** > **Basic Info**, verify the start and end times. Students can only enter after the start time, and submissions close at the end time. Ensure the server's time zone matches your institutional location.

Next, review your chosen policies:

- Whether Exam Mode and proctoring logs are truly necessary for this evaluation.
- Whether rejoining is permitted after network interruptions.
- Whether physical QR check-in equipment and screen projection are available on-site.
- Whether students have been informed about supported devices, browsers, and network requirements.

If Exam Integrity is enabled, the platform flags anomalous events and evidence but never automatically penalizes students. Proctors must understand response guidelines beforehand and retain human review after the exam.

## Pre-Flight Checklist

Teaching teams can use this checklist for final verification:

- [ ] Teacher account and co-instructor / TA privileges are configured correctly.
- [ ] Classroom roster matches the intended participant list for this exam.
- [ ] Question order, types, answer keys, individual points, and total score are accurate.
- [ ] Open-ended questions contain rubrics usable by both instructors and TAs.
- [ ] Images, code blocks, tables, and math formulas render properly in Demo Mode.
- [ ] Start time, end time, and server time zone are properly set.
- [ ] Exam mode, rejoining, and QR check-in align with on-site policies.
- [ ] Exam guidelines clearly state device, browser, and network requirements for students.

Completing this checklist confirms that QJudge is fully prepared for your first exam. Student test-taking, proctoring exception handling, grading, and grade publication will be covered in subsequent operational guides.
